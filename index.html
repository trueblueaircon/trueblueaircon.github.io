THE ACTUAL PROBLEM

That musty smell isn't normal.

Dirty filters and dust can restrict airflow over time — your unit works harder to cool the same room, and the air moving through it picks up that musty smell.

It builds up slowly, so it's easy to miss until it's obvious. A proper clean gets ahead of it before it affects how the unit runs.

WHAT WE ACTUALLY DO

What happens during a clean

No shortcuts — here's the actual sequence, every visit.

01

Check the filters

We pull the filter panels and check the real condition — dust load, mould spotting, wear — before anything else.

02

Clean the coils properly

The coils are where most of the build-up actually sits. We use the right tools to get in there, not just a wipe-down of what's visible.

03

Get the details a quick clean misses

Drainage, casing, the areas around the fan — the spots that get skipped when someone's rushing.

04

Test it running

We run the unit before we go, so you know it's actually working properly — not just looking clean.
